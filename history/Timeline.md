## CALENDAR & DATING SYSTEM

> How does this world measure time? What epoch does the calendar count from?

### Parallel Timelines

Three timelines run simultaneously (Canon #21). All events should carry a date in each applicable timeline.

| Timeline | Abbreviation | Epoch / Year Zero | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Universal | U.Y. (Universal Year) | The First Coalescence (Canon #2) | Tracks all events since the first spark. Exact numerical values for early periods are ambiguous — the scale is cosmological. |
| Emora | E.Y. (Emora Year) | Year 0 = tens of thousands of U.Y. in | Tracks all events on the planet Emora in one continuous timeline from the world's formation. |
| Modern | TBD | Year 0 = roughly 4,000–7,000 E.Y. in | Tracks events since the start of the Current Era. Abbreviation and year-naming convention TBD — must relate to an in-world event at the Current Era's opening. |

### In-World Calendar

- **Days:** Different names from real-world Mon–Sun. Names TBD.
- **Months:** Entirely different from real-world months. Names and count TBD.
- **Days per year:** Different from 365/366. Structure should incorporate 16 as a thematically significant number (Canon #22; see also Canon #8 — the 16-orb division cycle).
- **World Anvil integration:** A "real world normalized" calendar (Mon–Sun) will exist alongside the in-world calendar for player reference. World Anvil supports multiple simultaneous calendars.

### Notes on Dating Reliability

- Pre-Emora events (Genesis and Emergence Eras) are U.Y. only — no Emoran record exists for this period.
- Planar War Era and Dawning Era records may be fragmentary or suppressed.
- The Unnamed Era's records are unknown in quality — may be sparse if the era ended in near-extinction.
- Modern Era records are the most reliable.
