## Treaty of the Dawn
**Era:** Dawning Era
**Universal Year:** TBD — beginning of the Dawning Era
**Emora Year:** TBD
**Summary:** The magical founding agreement of the Dawning Era. Imposes a mark on all interplanar travelers preventing them from harming or exploiting inhabitants of the destination plane. Applies to all planes and all directions of travel.
**Canon / Glossary Reference:** Canon #19; Glossary "Treaty of the Dawn"
**Open Questions:**
- What exactly the mark looks like or does beyond restricting aggression.
- Who enforces violations — an interplanar authority, or self-enforcing magic.
- Known loopholes, or factions who claim to have found them — cross-reference powers/Factions.md.
- Location of the treaty's founding is unknown — possibly Yanuhfroh, possibly a now-lost location.
- Cause: the devastation of the Planar War Era.
- Consequence: all interplanar travel now carries the mark; Yanuhfroh becomes (or formalizes) its role as the primary regulated hub for Mortal Plane interplanar travel; certain factions actively work to circumvent the treaty.
