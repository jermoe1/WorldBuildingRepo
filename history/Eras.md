## Genesis Era
**In-World Name:** ⚠️ NAME PENDING
**Universal Year Range:** U.Y. 0 – [The Collision] *(duration not meaningfully measurable — time as a concept did not exist before the planes formed; Canon #27)*
**Emora Year Range:** N/A — pre-dates Emora's existence
**Trigger Event:** The Spark / The First Coalescence (Canon #2, #29)
**Close Event:** The Collision (Canon #11, #26)
**Defining Characteristics:**
- The primordial period. Raw mana crystallizes from the Astral Sea at The Spark, forming the Primordial Monolith.
- The Monolith grows by consuming Loom-Clouds and progresses through developmental thresholds (Canon #3–#5).
- Entities divide upon reaching 16 Cassel Spheres, repeating across the Four Schisms (Canon #8, #31) until 16 distinct primordial entities exist.
- The Equilibrium Period (Canon #32): the 16 entities fill the Astral Sea completely and press against each other in sustained low-level friction.
- All Genesis Era knowledge is reconstructed inference — no witness existed to record these events. Primary scholarly authority: the Collegium of Primordial Records (active in the [Unnamed Era]).
**Open Questions:**
- In-world name for the era itself.
- In-world name for The First Coalescence / The Spark event terminology distinctions.
**Canon References:** #1–#12, #25–#32, #34–#37 (full taxonomy)

## Dawning Era
**In-World Name:** ⚠️ NAME PENDING
**Universal Year Range:** [Treaty of the Dawn] – [Divine beings' separation event]
**Emora Year Range:** TBD
**Trigger Event:** Treaty of the Dawn (Canon #19)
**Close Event:** Divine beings separate to the Divine Realm (Canon #13)
**Defining Characteristics:**
- A new order enforced by the Treaty of the Dawn. The Divine beings oversee a period of relative stability.
- Ends when the Divinities, having seen success in creating and maintaining their planes, set in motion a plan to "unshackle their realms in the pursuit of true freedom and free will" — powerful combined magics bind all planar travelers to laws preventing substantial conflict, and the gods withdraw.
