### [Sword of Damocles]
**Also Known As:** *(requires an in-world name — "Sword of Damocles" is a real-world reference)*
**Type:** Ritual / Artifact Concept / World-Level Event
**Origin:** Created at some point (past or future) by a powerful mage or group of mages via a completed ritual
**Current Location:** N/A — not a physical object; the artifact is the ritual state and its effects on Candidates
**Current Possessor:** N/A
**Status:** Latent — activated by the completion of the ritual

**Description**
Not a sword. The "Sword of Damocles" refers to a ritual state — the marks placed on Candidates by the essence of Emora itself, and the gathering event they trigger.

**Properties & Capabilities**
A ritual identifies Candidates worthy in some specific aspect (strength, intelligence, dedication, bravery, or other virtues). The essence of Emora finds these individuals worthy and marks them on their body. Each mark grants a magical boon related to the aspect the Candidate was chosen for. Candidates feel a faint, initially resistible compulsion to travel toward a specific landmark. The compulsion grows stronger over time — weeks to months to years, at DM's pace. When all **living** Candidates are within 1,000 meters of each other, an event triggers.

Design inspirations: Anime *Project K*; Anime *Rokka no Yuusha*

*(unknown — does the mark carry a cost beyond the compulsion?)*

**History** *(unknown — when was/will this ritual be performed? Is it cyclical or a singular event?)*

**Myths & Legends** *(unknown)*

**Why It Matters**
A world-level event in waiting. The convergence of all living Candidates is effectively inevitable unless Candidates die before it can occur.
