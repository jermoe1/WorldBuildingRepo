### Partial Language Proficiency
**Replaces / Modifies:** Standard binary language proficiency (know it or don't)
**In Brief:** Characters who grew up near another language community may have rudimentary proficiency — enough to catch phrases, not to be fluent.
**Full Rule:** *(TBD — define tiers: passive comprehension only / basic spoken phrases / limited literacy)*
**Fictional Justification:** People in real life regularly pick up fragments of neighboring languages without achieving fluency. A Gnome raised near Elven speakers might recognize common phrases or read simple Elven text, even without formal training.
**Playtest Notes:** Untested — mechanic not yet defined.

---

### Level 0 — Pre-Adventurer Class
**Replaces / Modifies:** Standard Level 1 as entry point
**In Brief:** Characters begin as "powerful citizens" at Level 0, bridging the gap between an ordinary person and a Level 1 adventurer.
**Full Rule:** *(TBD — define what a Level 0 character can and cannot do; how progression to Level 1 occurs)*
**Fictional Justification:** A first-level adventurer is significantly more capable than an ordinary civilian, with no narrative explanation for why. Level 0 represents the accumulated skills and experiences of a capable adult life in this world — before formal adventurer training or the circumstances that trigger it.
**Playtest Notes:** Untested — concept only.
