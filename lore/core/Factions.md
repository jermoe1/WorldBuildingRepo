## HOW TO USE THIS FILE

Each faction gets its own entry block. Factions range from world-spanning empires to local gangs — scale is noted in the Type field. When a faction is referenced in another file, it should have an entry here. The Relationships section of each entry should be kept in sync with Relationships.md.

### Entry Template

```
### [Faction Name]
**Type:** Nation / Empire / City-State / Religious Order / Cult / Guild / Criminal Organization / Army / Other
**Scale:** Local / Regional / Continental / Global
**Headquarters / Base of Operations:** [Cross-reference Geography.md]
**Patron Deity (if any):** [Cross-reference Gods.md]
**Alignment (Narrative):** [1–2 words describing moral character — not a game stat]

**Purpose & Goals**
[What does this faction want? What are they working toward? Short-term and long-term.]

**Structure & Leadership**
[How is this faction organized? Who leads it? How is power distributed or contested?]

**Key Figures**
[Named leaders, champions, rivals within the faction — cross-reference NPCs.md]

**Membership**
[Who joins this faction? What are the entry requirements? What do members believe or value?]

**Resources & Capabilities**
[What does this faction control — armies, wealth, knowledge, territory, magical assets?]

**Public Face vs. Reality**
[How does this faction present itself, and what is it actually doing?]

**Relationships**
- **Allied with:** [Cross-reference other factions and Relationships.md]
- **In conflict with:**
- **Complicated relationship with:**
- **Patron of / Subordinate to:**

[How did this faction form? Key events in its past. Cross-reference Timeline.md.]

[What is this faction doing right now? What pressures are they under?]

**Notes & Open Questions:**
```
