## HOW TO USE THIS FILE

The Timeline is organized by **Era** first, then by **Event** within each era. Dates are relative to whatever dating system the world uses — establish the calendar and epoch before adding many entries. Events should include cause and consequence, not just bare facts. When an event is mentioned in another file, it should have an entry here.

## CALENDAR & DATING SYSTEM

> How does this world measure time? What epoch does the calendar count from?

### Parallel Timelines

Three timelines run simultaneously (Canon #21). All events should carry a date in each applicable timeline.

| Timeline | Abbreviation | Epoch / Year Zero | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Universal | U.Y. (Universal Year) | The First Coalescence (Canon #2) | Tracks all events since the first spark. Exact numerical values for early periods are ambiguous — the scale is cosmological. |
| Emora | E.Y. (Emora Year) | Year 0 = tens of thousands of U.Y. in | Tracks all events on the planet Emora in one continuous timeline from the world's formation. |
| Modern | TBD | Year 0 = roughly 4,000–7,000 E.Y. in | Tracks events since the start of the Current Era. Abbreviation and year-naming convention TBD — must relate to an in-world event at the Current Era's opening. |

### In-World Calendar

- **Days:** Different names from real-world Mon–Sun. Names TBD.
- **Months:** Entirely different from real-world months. Names and count TBD.
- **Days per year:** Different from 365/366. Structure should incorporate 16 as a thematically significant number (Canon #22; see also Canon #8 — the 16-orb division cycle).
- **World Anvil integration:** A "real world normalized" calendar (Mon–Sun) will exist alongside the in-world calendar for player reference. World Anvil supports multiple simultaneous calendars.

### Notes on Dating Reliability

- Pre-Emora events (Genesis and Emergence Eras) are U.Y. only — no Emoran record exists for this period.
- Planar War Era and Dawning Era records may be fragmentary or suppressed.
- The Unnamed Era's records are unknown in quality — may be sparse if the era ended in near-extinction.
- Modern Era records are the most reliable.

### Genesis Era
**Approximate Dates:** U.Y. 0 – [The Collision]
**Character:** The primordial period. Raw mana crystallizes from the Astral Sea, entities form and grow through developmental thresholds, divide upon reaching 16 memory orbs, and repeat until 16 distinct primordial entities fill the Astral Sea and collide.
**Key Events:** The First Coalescence; Threshold events (1, 2, 3); the 16-entity genealogical cycle; The Collision
**How It Ended:** The Collision — the 16 primordial entities made contact, producing a cascading explosion that formed the first planes (Canon #11).
**Cross-reference:** Canon #1–#12; Gods.md (pre-pantheon genealogical tree)

### Dawning Era
**Approximate Dates:** [Treaty of the Dawn] – [Divine beings' separation event]
**Character:** A new order enforced by the Treaty of the Dawn. The Divine beings oversee a period of relative stability. Ends when the Divinities, having seen success in the creation and maintenance of their planes, set in motion a plan to "unshackle their realms in the pursuit of true freedom and free will" — powerful combined magics bind all planar travelers to laws preventing substantial conflict, and the gods withdraw.
**Key Events:** Treaty of the Dawn; Planar Acquiescence *(TBD)*; Pact of Shadows *(TBD)*; Ilketh Proclamation *(TBD)*; Divine separation event
**How It Ended:** The separation of the Divine beings — their withdrawal into the Divine Realm or some isolated state, leaving the other planes to operate without direct divine governance.
**Cross-reference:** Treaty of the Dawn (Events section below)

### Treaty of the Dawn
**Era:** Dawning Era
**Date:** *(TBD — beginning of the Dawning Era)*
**Location:** *(unknown — possibly Yanuhfroh, or a now-lost location)*
**Parties Involved:** Divine beings; Mythical beings; possibly mortal representatives *(TBD)*

**What Happened**
The magical founding agreement of the Dawning Era (Canon #19). A binding treaty that imposes a mark on all travelers who cross between planes. The mark prevents the bearer from going on a rampage or taking advantage of the inhabitants of the destination plane. The treaty applies to all planes and all directions of travel — not only crossings into the Mortal Plane.

**Cause**
The devastation of the Planar War Era — a period of unchecked interplanar invasion and conflict.

**Consequence**
- All interplanar travel now carries the mark and its restrictions.
- Yanuhfroh becomes (or formalized its role as) the primary regulated hub for Mortal Plane interplanar travel.
- Certain factions are actively working to circumvent the treaty for their own benefit.

**In-World Sources** *(unknown)*

**Notes & Open Questions:**
- What exactly does "the mark" look like or do beyond restricting aggression? *(Thematic reference: The Shadow of What Was Lost by James Islington — not source material)*
- Who enforces treaty violations? Is there an interplanar authority, or does the magic self-enforce?
- Are there known loopholes — or factions who claim to have found them?
- Cross-reference: Factions.md (any faction seeking to circumvent the treaty)
