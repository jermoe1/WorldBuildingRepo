## HOW TO USE THIS FILE

"Peoples" covers both biological categories (races, species) and cultural groupings (ethnicities, civilizations) — the distinction between the two should be noted in each entry. In a dark fantasy world, origins and histories are often violent or tragic; document that honestly. Avoid presenting any people as monolithic — note internal diversity where it exists.
