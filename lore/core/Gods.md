# Gods.md
> Pantheon entries, divine domains, relationships between gods, worship practices, and current status.
> All entries must be consistent with the Canon Registry in WorldBuildingAssistant.md.
> Cross-reference: Factions.md (religious factions), Relationships.md (divine relationships), Timeline.md (death/ascension events).

---

## HOW TO USE THIS FILE

Each god gets its own entry block. Use the template below. When a god is mentioned in another file, link back here. Status must be kept current — if a god dies or goes dormant, update the Status field and log the event in Timeline.md.

### Entry Template

```
### [God's Name]
**Domain(s):** [What this god rules over — be specific]
**Also Known As:** [Epithets, alternate names, names in other cultures]
**Status:** Active / Dormant / Dead / Unknown
**Alignment (Narrative):** [Not a game stat — describe their moral character in 1–2 words]

**Nature & Character**
[What kind of being is this god? How do they relate to mortals and to other gods?]

**Divine Domain in Practice**
[What does this god's power actually look like in the world? What phenomena or forces are attributed to them?]

**Worship**
- **Clergy:** [Who serves this god? How are they organized?]
- **Rituals:** [Key practices, holy days, offerings]
- **Temples / Holy Sites:** [Where is this god venerated?]
- **Forbidden Acts:** [What does this god prohibit or punish?]

**Relationships**
- **Allied with:** [Other gods — cross-reference Gods.md and Relationships.md]
- **In conflict with:** [Other gods]
- **Patron of:** [Factions, peoples, or places — cross-reference Factions.md, Peoples.md]

**History & Myth**
[Key events in this god's history. What stories are told about them? Cross-reference Timeline.md.]

**Current Status & Activity**
[What is this god doing now, if anything? Are they present in the world? Silent? Absent?]

**Notes & Open Questions**
[Anything unresolved, speculative, or flagged for further development]
```
