## CASSEL SPHERE DEVELOPMENT CYCLE

> The biological mechanism underlying every schism. See Canon #5–#10, #31.

1. A mana entity accumulates Cassel Spheres through growth.
2. Upon reaching 16 Cassel Spheres, the spheres migrate to a central position and arrange in a symmetrical circle.
3. A sorting process of unknown mechanism occurs.
4. The entity divides into two halves of 8 Cassel Spheres each.
5. Each half retains a portion of the original's personality/neural pattern and diverges in behavior from its sibling.

This cycle repeated four times (the Four Schisms) to produce the 16 primordial entities from the single Primordial Monolith.

**Open Questions:**
- The mechanism of the sorting process (step 3) is unknown.
- Whether 16 has cosmological significance beyond this biological fact is unknown (Canon #28).
